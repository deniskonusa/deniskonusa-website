Signal Sec

Branding for a global crypto security conference.

The core idea of the identity is based on the concept of signal and noise. In a complex decentralized environment, security systems must identify real threats and filter out irrelevant data. The conference explores technologies that protect blockchain infrastructure and digital assets.

The visual identity is built around a pixel-based symbol derived from the letter S, representing a digital signal emerging from noise. This element forms the foundation of the entire design system and is used across all visual communications — including posters, digital platforms, presentation materials, merchandise, and conference environment graphics.

The goal of the project was to develop a cohesive design system and a complete communication framework that ensures visual consistency across all touchpoints of the event.
