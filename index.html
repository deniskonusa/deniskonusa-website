Questions, answered.

Why exactly 14 days?

The fixed timeline is what makes the sprint work. Deadlines eliminate endless revision cycles and force decisive choices. We move fast — and so do you.

What do I need to provide?

A 60–90 minute kickoff call, feedback at two key milestones, and access to your company materials. We handle everything else.

How many revisions are included?

One round of revisions per stage. The constraints keep focus sharp — that's how strong results happen, not compromises.

Is naming a separate service?

Naming is an optional add-on within the sprint. If you already have a name, we simply skip that step. If you need one, we build it in.

In what format are deliverables handed over?

Organized Figma file with all frames, SVG/PNG/PDF exports, shareable brand book link, and a published website ready to go live.

What if I need more than 6 website pages?

Design Sprint is your foundation. For most startups, 6 pages is exactly what's needed at launch. If your scope grows, we can discuss an extended engagement after delivery.

Ready to launch your brand in 14 days?

Tell us about your project. We'll figure out together if a sprint is the right fit — no commitment required.
